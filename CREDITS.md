Everything else is generated by the scripts in `blender/`: the house, terrain,
palms, agaves, shrubs, rocks and curtains, and in the dining room the table,
chairs, pendant lamps, planters, chessboard, rug and the painting. The painting
is an original abstract made by `dining.paint_painting`, not a copy of any work.

On the pool deck everything is generated too (`blender/villa/pool.py`): the
loungers, parasol, side table, sofas, fire pit, planters, lanterns, towels,
sunglasses, the sake bottle and cup, the martini glass and the contact card. The sake label is an original design (`labels.paint_sake_label`): the
brewery name "Sunakaze" is made up, and the ensō and the seal are painted by
the script.

The visitor's hand and the loafers (`blender/villa/frozen/`) were made from
scratch by script too: no character generator, scan or downloaded model.

The game on the board is a standard opening line (Ruy Lopez, Chigorin
variation), which nobody owns.

In the study (`blender/villa/study.py`) everything but the two plants is generated:
the sofa, desk, chair, credenza, bookshelf and books, lamps, coffee table,
whiteboard, journal and cup. The Persian-style rug is an original design painted by
`study.paint_rug`, not a copy of any rug. The whiteboard and the journal are
lettered with Kalam (see Fonts).

In the games room (`blender/villa/games.py`) everything but the plant is generated:
the desk, monitors and arm, keyboard, mouse, PC, chair, shelf, game cases, panels,
sofa, table and blind. The screens, the game cases' spines and the framed print are
painted by the script. The print is an original landscape, not a photograph. The
game titles are set in plain type; no logos, cover art or screenshots are used, and
the chart's two team names are placeholders.

In the kitchen (`blender/villa/kitchen.py`) everything but the two plants is generated:
the cabinets, island, shelves, stools, pendants, espresso machine, jars, canisters
and tins, bowls, lemons and the tiles (painted by `kitchen.paint_tiles`). The jar
labels are the tool names from `data/kitchen_labels.txt`, set in plain type
(`kitchen.paint_labels`); no logos are used.

In the gym (`blender/villa/gym.py`) everything is generated: the mats, the heavy bag
and its chains, the speed bag and platform, the gloves, duffel bag, shin guards, hand
wraps, bottle, bench, light bars, jump rope and the round timer, whose digits are
set in plain type.

In the living room (`blender/villa/living.py`) everything but the fig is generated: the
sofa, rug, coffee table, TV and its stand, bench, lamp, frames, and the whole tank (its
rocks and moss, driftwood, plants and fish). The TV's paused screen is painted by the
script, with "Hunter × Hunter" in plain type: no logo, artwork or screenshot. The tank
has a plain moss-covered rock where the original mech will go.

The moodboard's pictures are your own choice, supplied in `assets_src/moodboard/` and
shrunk into `web/assets/moodboard/` by `npm run moodboard`. They are the one exception
to the plain-type rule (`BUILD_BRIEF.md`); any rights in them belong to their owners,
and they can be swapped or removed by changing that folder and running the script.

In the library (`blender/villa/library.py`) everything is generated: the shelving,
ladder, books, desk, chairs, lamps, laptop, manuscript and the rug (an original
design). The real titles (`data/library_books.txt`) are set in plain type on plain
spines (`library.paint_spines`), in Inter, Crimson Text and UnifrakturMaguntia (see
Fonts); no cover art or publishers' marks are used. The typed pages on the desk and
the laptop are shapes standing in for text, not words.

In the trophy room (`blender/villa/trophy.py`) everything but the chess pieces and the
fig is generated: the glass cases, the belt (an original design: no real title's belt
or sanctioning body's mark), the medals and their ribbons, the cup and the other awards,
the console, the bench, the chess table and board, the chairs, the rug and the ceiling
track. The team photo's frame is empty for now.

In the main bedroom (`blender/villa/bedroom.py`) everything is generated: the bed and its
linen, the headboard, the nightstand, the lamps, the shelf and its books, and the rug.
The world map is an original print painted by `bedroom.paint_map` from Natural Earth's
coastlines (see Map data below); the pins are modelled. The books' spines are plain
type from `data/bedroom_books.txt`.

In the garage (`blender/villa/garage.py`) everything is generated: the floor and its
lines, the light fittings, the key cabinet and keys, the screen, the charger and the
extinguisher. The key tags and the booking calendar are painted by the script in plain
type from `data/garage_keys.txt`; the bookings on the calendar are made up.

In the foyer (`blender/villa/foyer.py`) everything but the fig is generated: the plinth,
the console and what's on it, the stairs' glass balustrade and rail, and the runner.
The painting over the console is an original abstract made by `foyer.paint_painting`,
not a copy of any work.

## The aquarium mech

The mech in the living room's tank is built from a downloaded model (`MECH_MODEL` in
`blender/villa/config.py`). The download stays in `assets_src/` (not committed); the
build thins it, poses it, repaints it and adds moss, so only that changed copy ships,
inside `web/assets/rooms/living.glb`. The living room's panel shows the credit.
It is fan art in the style of a well-known franchise; you chose to use it (20 Nov
2026) and to take it down on request. To do that, set `MECH_MODEL = None`, rebuild,
and delete this row.
