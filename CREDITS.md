## Models

From [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com), license CC0. Downloaded as glTF with
1k textures by `blender/fetch_assets.py` into `assets_src/models/` (not
committed). The build keeps their shape and colour map, thins the densest
meshes, and leaves out the parts it replaces (the plants' own pots and soil).

| Pool deck: the trees in the planters | Quiver Tree 02 | Rico Cilliers (model), Dario Barresi (photography) | CC0 | https://polyhaven.com/a/quiver_tree_02 |

Everything else is generated by the scripts in `blender/`: the house, terrain,
palms, agaves, shrubs, rocks and curtains, and in the dining room the table,
chairs, pendant lamps, planters, chessboard, rug and the painting. The painting
is an original abstract made by `dining.paint_painting`, not a copy of any work.

On the pool deck everything is generated too (`blender/villa/pool.py`): the
loungers, parasol, side table, sofas, fire pit, planters, lanterns, towels,
sunglasses, the sake bottle and cup, the martini glass and the contact card. The sake label is an original design (`labels.paint_sake_label`): the
brewery name "Sunakaze" is made up, and the ensō and the seal are painted by
the script.

The visitor's hand and the loafers (`blender/villa/frozen/`) were made from
scratch by script too: no character generator, scan or downloaded model.

The game on the board is a standard opening line (Ruy Lopez, Chigorin
variation), which nobody owns.
